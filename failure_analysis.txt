### RELATION TO POWERFLOWS AND PNM

**PowerFlows v0.15 Issues:**
- Reactive power limit warnings appear frequently (benign)
- Test 35: Actual PF solver failure (bus configuration)
- Otherwise: PF solves successfully but produces different operating points vs. PSY3/PF0.6

**PowerNetworkMatrices v0.18.2:**
- No direct failures attributed to PNM
- Ybus construction warnings appear but don't cause failures

**Root Cause:**
- Primary issue: Operating points from Newton-Raphson PF differ from old NLSolve PF
- This causes dynamic models to initialize at different states
- Some dynamic models cannot converge at new operating points (init failures)
- Some systems become dynamically unstable at new operating points (simulation failures)
